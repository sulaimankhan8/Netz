# NETZ new units: basic and general maths taxonomy

Research date: 2026-11-03. Scope: global plus India. This covers three new units (Calculus, Algebra, Geometry & Trigonometry), each broken into sub-parts, then topics, then the computable methods a step-by-step solver can show. Each part has demand signals and the curriculum it maps to.

---

## 0. How to read the demand numbers

- **Keyword volume** comes from seodata.dev, which says its figures are a "monthly average for US, from the Google Ads API". These are US-only figures, and each page shows its own update date (May–Oct 2026). Global volume is usually 2–4× the US figure for maths keywords. India is usually the #2–#3 country (see site shares below).
- **Site-level keyword and traffic data** comes from Ahrefs public pages (ahrefstop.com, data for Oct 2026). These pages show only each site's top 5 US keywords.
- Semrush and Similarweb pages for page-level data (Top Pages) need a login. No page-level breakdown of Symbolab, Mathway or calculator.net was publicly available.
- Google Trends wasn't fetched. Use Trends to compare India against the world before committing (a to-do for the owner).
- All volumes are estimates. Tools disagree by ±30–50%. Use them to rank, not as exact numbers.

## 3. UNIT A: CALCULUS

Demand: high. "integral calculator" (165K US) and "derivative calculator" (135K US) are the two highest-volume method keywords in all three units.

### A1. Limits & continuity
Curricula: NCERT 11 ch13, 12 ch5; JEE u7; AP unit 1; A-level (limits are informal there)
- Evaluate a limit by direct substitution
- Factor and cancel (0/0)
- Rationalise (conjugate)
- Standard limits (sin x/x, (1+1/x)^x, (aˣ−1)/x)
- **L'Hôpital's rule** (0/0, ∞/∞, plus the 0·∞, ∞−∞, 1^∞, 0⁰, ∞⁰ forms)
- Limits at infinity (divide by the highest power)
- One-sided limits and piecewise functions
- Squeeze theorem
- Continuity check at a point; types of discontinuity
- Asymptotes (vertical, horizontal, oblique)
- ε–δ proof (university only; low priority)
- Demand: "limit calculator" 33.1K US (variants 60.5K) https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/limit-calculator ; Symbolab ranks #1 for it (63K traffic)

### A2. Differentiation
Curricula: NCERT 11 ch13, 12 ch5; JEE u7; AP units 2–3; A-level
- From first principles (difference quotient)
- Power, sum and constant rules
- **Product rule**, **quotient rule**, **chain rule**
- Derivatives of trig, inverse trig, exponential and log functions
- **Implicit differentiation**
- **Logarithmic differentiation**
- Parametric derivatives (dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt))
- Derivative of an inverse function
- Higher-order derivatives (second derivative and beyond)
- Partial derivatives (Calc III)
- Demand: "derivative calculator" 135K US; "derivative solver" 135K https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/derivative-calculator ; derivative-calculator.net had about 165K for that term on Semrush (https://ja.semrush.com/website/derivative-calculator.net/overview)

### A3. Applications of derivatives
Curricula: NCERT 12 ch6; AP units 4–5; JEE
- Tangent and normal line equations
- Rate of change; **related rates**
- Increasing/decreasing intervals
- **Critical points**; first and second derivative tests; maxima and minima
- **Optimisation** word problems
- Concavity and inflection points
- Curve sketching (full analysis)
- Mean value theorem and Rolle's theorem
- Linear approximation and differentials; Newton's method (A-level numerical methods)
- Demand: medium. eMathHelp has a separate page for each of these; Symbolab groups them as "Derivative Applications".

### A4. Integration (techniques)
Curricula: NCERT 12 ch7; JEE u8; AP unit 6; A-level; Calc II
- Basic antiderivatives (the reverse power rule, standard forms)
- **u-substitution**
- **Integration by parts** (including the tabular method and the LIATE order)
- **Partial fractions** (linear, repeated and irreducible-quadratic factors)
- **Trig substitution** (a²−x², a²+x², x²−a²)
- Trig integrals (powers of sin/cos, tan/sec)
- Special NCERT forms: ∫dx/(x²±a²), ∫√(a²−x²), ∫eˣ(f+f′)
- **Definite integrals** and the fundamental theorem of calculus; properties (King's rule, even/odd)
- Definite integral as the limit of a sum (NCERT)
- **Improper integrals** (convergence)
- Numerical: Riemann sums, trapezoid rule, Simpson's rule
- Double and triple integrals (Calc III)
- Demand: "integral calculator" 165K US (variants 246K) https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/integral-calculator ; "integration by parts" 32K (mathsisfun ranks #1); "antiderivative calculator" 18K; "double integral calculator" 19K

### A5. Applications of integrals
Curricula: NCERT 12 ch8; AP unit 8; Calc II
- **Area under a curve and area between curves**
- **Volume of revolution** (disc, washer and shell methods)
- **Arc length**
- Surface area of revolution
- Average value of a function
- Motion: displacement vs distance from velocity

### A6. Differential equations
Curricula: NCERT 12 ch9; JEE u9; AP unit 7; A-level
- Order and degree
- Variable separable
- Homogeneous equations
- Linear first-order (integrating factor)
- Exact equations
- Second-order with constant coefficients (university)
- Euler's method; slope fields (AP)
- Laplace transforms (university; low priority)

### A7. Sequences & series (calculus)
Curricula: AP BC unit 10; Calc II; A-level Further
- Convergence tests: nth-term, geometric, p-series, integral, comparison, limit comparison, ratio, root, alternating series
- Radius and interval of convergence
- **Taylor/Maclaurin polynomials and series**; remainder (Lagrange) error bound
- Power-series operations
- Demand: "radius of convergence calculator" 25K US (Symbolab #1); "taylor series calculator" 5.4K (variants 12.1K) https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/taylor-series-calculator

### A8. Parametric, polar & multivariable (BC / Calc III; later)
- Parametric dy/dx and arc length
- Polar area
- Gradient and directional derivative
- Lagrange multipliers
- Line integrals
- Green's theorem
- Demand: low except "double integral calculator" (19K)

## 7. Suggested build order for NETZ (ranked by demand, then step-by-step value)

1. **Calculus:** derivative (with the rule chain shown) → integral (substitution / by parts / partial fractions) → limits (including L'Hôpital) → applications (tangent, extrema, area between curves).
2. **Algebra:** quadratic (formula, factoring, completing the square) → linear systems (substitution, elimination, Cramer, matrix) → factoring and polynomial division → matrices and determinants → logs and exponents → inequalities.
3. **Geometry & Trig:** triangle solver (Pythagoras, SOH-CAH-TOA, sine and cosine rules) → coordinate tools (slope, distance, midpoint, line equations) → area and volume → unit circle and trig equations → conics → 3D lines and planes (for India).

Sub-parts with weak search demand but strong demand from Indian exams (still worth building for NCERT/JEE learners): P&C, binomial, AP/GP, complex numbers, differential equations, 3D geometry, vectors.
