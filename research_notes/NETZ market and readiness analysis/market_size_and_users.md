## How many Indian students study engineering/STEM, and how many take numerical methods each year? Which universities teach it, and in which semester?

### Takeaway
India has about 14 lakh students entering B.E./B.Tech each year, and about 37 lakh students enrolled in B.Tech/BE in total. Every major state technical university checked has a compulsory numerical-methods course, usually in semester 2–4. So nearly every engineering student takes one numerical-methods paper. That means the first-year group sitting such a course each year is about 12–19 lakh students, based on enrolment rising from 14.1 lakh (2021-22) toward the 75% fill rate in 2024-25.

### Cited Findings
**Enrolment and intake**
- AICTE data for 2021-22: approved B.E./B.Tech intake was 25,39,502, enrolment was 14,11,001 and 11,28,501 seats stayed vacant — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/btech-engineering-college-aicte-data-course-placement-admission-graduation-aishe-vacant-seats)
- According to AISHE, BTech/BE enrolment (all years) fell 10.3%, from 40,85,321 to 36,63,685, between 2016 and 2021 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/btech-engineering-college-aicte-data-course-placement-admission-graduation-aishe-vacant-seats)
- In AISHE 2021-22, Engineering & Technology makes up 11.8% of undergraduate enrolment. The other shares are Arts 34.2%, Science 14.8% and Commerce 13.3% — [AISHE 2021-22 summaries via Careers360 / Vision IAS](https://news.careers360.com/aishe-report-2022-highest-enrolment-in-ba-courses-maximum-phd-students-in-engineering-and-tech-stream/amp); full report: [AISHE 2021-22 PDF (education.gov.in)](https://education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/statistics-new/AISHE%20Book_2021-22_4.pdf)
- The AISHE 2020-21 report puts BE enrolment alone at 13.42 lakh. This counts BE only, not B.Tech — [AISHE coverage, Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/btech-engineering-college-aicte-data-course-placement-admission-graduation-aishe-vacant-seats)
- AISHE 2021-22 counts 9.2 lakh diploma pass-outs in total — [Careers360 / AISHE coverage](https://news.careers360.com/btech-engineering-college-aicte-data-course-placement-admission-graduation-aishe-vacant-seats/amp)

- The share of engineering seats filled (AICTE) rose from 53.73% (2020-21) to 65.45% (2021-22), 73.33% (2022-23), 73.40% (2023-24) and 75.07% (2024-25). AICTE calls 75% a five-year high. **Numerical methods in syllabi.** All of these come from third-party syllabus mirrors or college copies, not the official university PDFs.
- **Anna University (Tamil Nadu), R2021:** MA3251 *Statistics and Numerical Methods* is a **semester 2** course for CSE, IT, EEE and many other branches. It covers Gauss-Jordan and iterative methods (Gauss-Seidel), eigenvalues, interpolation, numerical differentiation and integration, Euler, modified Euler, RK4, and Milne/Adams-Bashforth predictor-corrector methods. The textbook is Grewal — [StuCor](https://stucor.in/syllabus/s-ma3251/), [InspireNignite CSE](https://www.inspirenignite.com/anna-university/ma3251-statistics-and-numerical-methods-syllabus-for-cse-2021-regulation/), [Padeepz](https://padeepz.net/ma3251-syllabus-statistics-and-numerical-methods-regulation-2021-anna-university/)
- **VTU (Karnataka):** 21MAT21 *Advanced Calculus and Numerical Methods* is a **semester 2** course (2021 scheme) for the Physics and Chemistry cycles, which means all first-year students. It covers Regula-Falsi, Newton-Raphson, Taylor series, modified Euler and RK4. Earlier versions are 18MAT21 (2018 scheme) and a 2017 scheme course. A 2025 scheme version, 25MAT21C (4 credits), also exists. The 2022 scheme has stream-specific Maths-II papers (BMATS201 for CSE, BMATM201 for ME) — [InspireNignite 21MAT21](https://www.inspirenignite.com/vtu/21mat21-advanced-calculus-and-numerical-methods-syllabus-physics-group-2021-scheme/), [SJBIT 25MAT21C PDF](https://sjbit.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Advanced-Calculus-and-Numerical-methods-_25MAT21C.pdf), [YIT BMATS201](https://yit.edu.in/storage/files/syllabus/BMATS201.pdf)
- **RGPV (Madhya Pradesh):** BT-401 *Mathematics-III* is a **semester 4** course. It has three numerical-methods units (bisection, Newton-Raphson, Regula Falsi, finite differences and interpolation, Euler, modified Euler, RK4, Milne and Adams predictor-corrector) plus transform calculus and probability — [Career Shiksha (RGPV IV sem)](https://career-shiksha.com/post/syllabus-of-btech-4th-sem-aids-rgpv/), [Career Shiksha BT-401](https://career-shiksha.com/post/important-rgpv-question-bt-401-m3-iv-sem-cse/)
- **GTU (Gujarat):** 2140706 *Numerical and Statistical Methods* is a **semester 4** course for Computer Engineering, IT and ICT. It covers numerical integration and ODEs and needs at least 10 lab programs. A separate 2140105 *Numerical Methods* course exists for Aeronautical (semester 4). Some GTU-affiliated colleges run *Probability, Statistics and Numerical Methods* (202000303) — [MBIT copy of GTU syllabus](https://www.mbit.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/syllabus/UGCP/2140706.pdf), [ADIT](https://www.adit.ac.in/assets/pdf/syllabus/202000303_Probability%20Statistics%20and%20Numerical%20Methods.pdf). BVM (an autonomous college in Gujarat) offers a *Computer Oriented Numerical Methods* course (2IT05) covering root-finding, curve fitting and matrices — [BVM](https://www.bvmengineering.ac.in/syllabi/UG1920/IT/2IT05.pdf)
- **AKTU (Uttar Pradesh):** KOE065 *Computer Based Numerical Techniques* is a **semester 7 open elective** for EE and ECE (2021-22 scheme). It covers Newton–Cotes, trapezoidal, Simpson 1/3 and 3/8, and Boole's rule. The textbooks are Jain–Iyengar–Jain and Grewal — [InspireNignite](https://www.inspirenignite.com/up/open-elective-1-koe065-computer-based-numerical-techniques-electrical-engineering-7th-sem-syllabus-for-aktu-b-tech-2021-22-scheme/)
- Pune-area colleges run numerical methods within TE (third-year) Mechanical 2019 course — [GCOE Ara TE Mech 2019 syllabus](https://mail.gcoeara.ac.in/syllabus/mech/2019_TE_Mechanical%20Engineering%20(2019%20Course).pdf). B.Sc and BCA programmes also include it, for example [Lingaya's B.Sc CS semester 4 BCS-208 Numerical Method](https://www.lingayasvidyapeeth.edu.in/sanmax/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Numerical-Method_BSc-4thsem_BCS-208.pdf), [KBN College BCA maths R20](https://mail.kbncollege.ac.in/attachments/R20BCA(MATHS)%20(1).pdf), [Dibrugarh University MCA elective](https://oldweb.dibru.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/MCA_General-Elective-Syllabus.pdf)
- The same textbooks keep coming up: B.S. Grewal *Numerical Methods in Engineering and Science* and Jain–Iyengar–Jain. NETZ's worked examples should follow their notation and methods.

### Inferences
- The yearly flow of students taking numerical methods is close to the whole engineering intake. VTU and Anna University teach it in semester 2 to every branch; RGPV and GTU teach it in semester 4. With about 14–19 lakh students entering B.E./B.Tech each year, roughly **12–18 lakh Indian engineering students sit a numerical-methods exam every year**. B.Sc Maths/Physics/CS, BCA, MCA and diploma students add to that, but I have no counts for them.
- Exams fall at the same times nationwide: semester-2 exams around May–July and semester-4 exams around May–June. Demand will spike at these times, so plan marketing and server capacity for them.
- Tamil Nadu (Anna University), Karnataka (VTU), Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana have the largest enrolments. Anna University and VTU teach numerical methods to every first-year student, which makes them the best first regions to target.

## Which exams use these topics (GATE, ESE, semester exams)?

### Takeaway
GATE 2025 had about 7–7.5 lakh candidates sit the exam. Engineering Mathematics, which includes numerical methods, is part of almost every GATE engineering paper. Sources disagree on the exact totals.

### Cited Findings
- GATE 2025 had 9,36,019 registered and 7,47,319 appeared — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/gate-cut-off-2025-drops-electrical-aerospace-engineering-qualifying-marks-gate2025-iitr-ac-in-how-many-candidates-applied-appeared/amp), [Zollege](https://zollege.in/news/gate-2025-cut-off-released-check-subject-wise-qualifying-marks-toppers-statistics-alertid-8055). **This conflicts with** about 8.6 lakh registered and about 6.9 lakh appeared — [IMS India](https://www.imsindia.com/?p=13687), [Physics Wallah](https://www.pw.live/gate/exams/gate-2025-statistical-and-performance-report). The official IIT Roorkee statistical report (mirror: [Collegedunia PDF](https://image-static.collegedunia.com/public/image/GATE2025StatisticalAndPerformanceReportWebVersion_8dc187042ef844faabca3e60a37ad34f.pdf)) should settle the difference.
- In GATE 2025, CS had 170,825 candidates appear (207,851 registered) — [Careers360](https://engineering.careers360.com/articles/how-many-students-appeared-for-gate-2025-branch-wise)

### Inferences
- GATE adds a market of about 7 lakh people a year. It overlaps with third- and final-year students and graduates, and in GATE numerical methods is only a small part of the roughly 13–15% Engineering Mathematics section. Semester exams are the bigger driver.

### Gaps
- I could not get the GATE 2026 numbers or the exact topic weights for numerical methods, so the 13–15% figure is background and unverified. I also found no candidate counts for ESE (UPSC IES) or state AE/JE exams.

## Indian edtech market size and trend, 2024–2026 (after Byju's)

### Takeaway
Funding has collapsed: Indian edtech raised $249M in 2025 according to Inc42, an eight-year low. Sector valuations vary widely between sources, about $2.8–8.9B. Analysts describe a shift from funding-led growth to profitable, model-led consolidation, including a wave of IPOs in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Indian edtech funding was **$249M in 2025 (down 56%)**, compared with **$568M in 2024** — [Inc42 (2024 figure)](https://inc42.com/?p=493221), [Inc42 2026 lists](https://inc42.com/lists/top-30-edtech-startups-in-india-2026-by-funding/)
- Tracxn: $214M raised in 2026 year-to-date, median round about $1.1M. Five of India's seven edtech IPOs ever happened between July and November 2025. Tracxn describes a sector moving "from funding-led growth to model-led consolidation" — [Entrepreneur India (Tracxn)](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/indias-edtech-sector-moves-from-funding-led-growth-to-model-led-consolidation-tracxn)
- Outlook Business reported a "5x funding surge in H1 2025", based on a small base of $18.5M across 11 rounds through April 2025. This conflicts with the Inc42 annual decline — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/investors/indian-edtech-start-ups-make-a-comeback-with-5x-funding-surge-in-h1-2025)
- IAMAI and Grant Thornton Bharat value the market at ₹64,875 crore ($7.5B) and project ₹2,50,850 crore ($29B) by 2030. IMARC estimates $2.8B in 2024, reaching $33.2B by 2033. MarketsandMarkets estimates about $8.88B in 2025 (edtech plus smart classrooms) with a 16.4% CAGR to 2030 — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-edtech-market/toc), [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/educational-technology-ed-tech-market/India)
- Ken Research sizes India edtech career programs at $2.5B (2025). It projects CAGRs of 15% for vocational/upskilling and 13% for test prep, compared with 8% for K-12 — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-edtech-career-programs-market)
- Redseer's older forecast put online higher education and lifelong learning at $0.5B then, growing to $4–6B by 2025. This was a forecast, not a measured figure — [Consultancy.in](https://www.consultancy.in/news/3307/online-higher-lifelong-education-could-reach-5-billion-by-2025)
- An explainer covers the post-Byju's context and rebound expectations — [The Week, Apr 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2025/04/11/india-edtech-segment-looks-to-make-a-rebound-despite-the-byjus-debacle-explainer.amp.html)

### Inferences
- Venture money for edtech is scarce, with small median rounds. A small, cheap product like NETZ (ads plus freemium in rupees) fits the current focus on unit economics better than a model that spends on growth.
- Market-size figures are not comparable with each other. Quote a range and name the source each time.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable split of spending on engineering coursework help (not test prep).

## How Indian engineering students study (YouTube, notes PDFs, Telegram) and their pain points

### Takeaway
Only indirect evidence was gathered. The large number of third-party sites mirroring syllabi, notes and question papers (StuCor, Padeepz, InspireNignite, Career Shiksha, Scribd, Studeersnel) shows that students rely heavily on free PDF notes and "important questions" lists organised by university and course code.

### Cited Findings
- Course-code-specific notes, question banks and "important questions" pages exist for MA3251 (Anna University) and BT-401 (RGPV) — [Padeepz MA3251 question bank](https://padeepz.net/ma3251-question-bank-statistics-and-numerical-methods-regulation-2021-anna-university/), [Padeepz notes](https://padeepz.net/ma3251-notes-statistics-and-numerical-methods-regulation-2021-anna-university), [StuCor notes/QP](https://stucor.in/notes-qp/ma3251/), [Career Shiksha important RGPV questions](https://career-shiksha.com/post/important-rgpv-question-bt-401-m3-iv-sem-cse/)
- Model question papers for VTU 21MAT21 are shared on Studeersnel — [Studeersnel](https://www.studeersnel.nl/ph/document/visvesvaraya-technological-university/engineering-mathematics/model-question-paper-for-21mat21-advanced-calculus-numerical-methods/155361900)
- VTU 21MAT21 asks only for the formulae of Regula-Falsi and Newton-Raphson, with no derivations. Exams test whether students can carry out the steps by hand — [InspireNignite](https://www.inspirenignite.com/vtu/21mat21-advanced-calculus-and-numerical-methods-syllabus-physics-group-2021-scheme/)

### Inferences
- **SEO opportunity:** students search by course code ("MA3251", "21MAT21", "BT-401", "2140706"). NETZ could rank with pages mapped to each code that link to its step-by-step method pages.
- Exams reward writing out each step and table by hand, not the final answer. That matches NETZ's step-by-step method pages and handwriting playground.
