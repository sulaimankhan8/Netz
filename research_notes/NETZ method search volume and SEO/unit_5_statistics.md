The figure is the 12-month total and the monthly average. Pageviews show worldwide topic interest. **They are not search volume.**

### Inferences
- **Priority order by US demand (estimate):** SD/variance, then confidence interval, mean-median-mode, z-score/normal, correlation, t-test, Bayes, ANOVA, chi-square, binomial, linear regression/line of best fit, then exponential distribution and expected value. The three Indian-syllabus pages (large-sample test, parabola fit, F-test) are the lowest US demand, but they match exam phrasings that Western calculator sites don't target.
- **"with steps" is a small modifier in the US** (for example "bayes theorem calculator with steps" 20, "covariance calculator with steps" 30). It still appears in India autocomplete for z-test, SD, correlation, regression, binomial, Poisson and Mann-Whitney, and it is NETZ's differentiator. Keep it in titles, but the head term must lead.
- **India phrasing differs from US phrasing.** India uses "fitting a straight line by least square method", "regression equation of x on y", "karl pearson coefficient of correlation", "large sample test for single mean", "skewness and kurtosis formula in terms of moments", "… in hindi" and "class 12" (Bayes). The US uses "line of best fit calculator", "linear regression calculator", "pearson correlation calculator" and "z test calculator". Each page should carry both.
- Casio fx-991ES/EX/CW suggestions for normal distribution point to Indian and other exam students checking calculator results. A "check with your Casio" note could serve that intent.
- Poisson, expected value, z-score and control chart have large non-student intent (betting, paediatrics, quality engineering). Their raw volumes overstate NETZ-relevant demand.

- **India and global volumes are missing for every keyword** (rate limit, and registration not done). To fill this in, the owner can run one batch with country=in in Google Keyword Planner or a registered seodata.dev key.
- US volumes are missing for f test, z test, large sample test, least squares (regression) calculator, quadratic/parabola regression, skewness and kurtosis calculators, Spearman, exponential/power regression, MGF, gamma/geometric/uniform calculators, Markov chain, one-way and two-way ANOVA calculators. seodata.dev had no cached page (404) and the API was rate-limited.
- Wikipedia pageviews for "Curve fitting" and "Joint probability distribution" could not be fetched (HTTP 429).

### Inferences
- **Cannibalisation risk:** `/least-squares`, `/fitting-straight-line` and the planned `/regression-lines` overlap on "linear regression / line of best fit". Suggested split: `/least-squares` is the general method hub (line and curve, "least squares method / formula"); `/fitting-straight-line` targets "line of best fit" and "fitting a straight line by least square method"; `/regression-lines` targets "regression equation of y on x / x on y". Each should link to the others.
- **z-score overlap:** `/normal-distribution` (z-score, area) and `/z-test` (hypothesis test) should cross-link. The z-test page should avoid "z score calculator" as its primary keyword, because that intent is mixed and largely medical.
- **Title convention:** the catalog example uses an em dash plus "NETZ". Where the 60-character limit bites, drop the brand rather than the head term (some titles above do this).

### Gaps
- Title and meta lengths were written to the stated limits but should be re-measured after final wording. Google truncates by pixel width (~580 px), not character count.
- The FAQ questions come from autocomplete and question-form keyword variants, not from scraped "People also ask" boxes, which I could not observe.
