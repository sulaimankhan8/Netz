**How to read the numbers**

- **"SD-US"** means seodata.dev, US, monthly average from the Google Ads API. Each figure came from that keyword's own page, `https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/<slug>` (for example [quadratic formula calculator](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/quadratic-formula-calculator)). Each page has its own "updated" date between 2026-05 and 2026-10, shown below as (upd MM-DD, 2026). Google Ads volumes are bucketed (880, 1.3K, 1.9K, 2.4K, 2.9K, 3.6K, 4.4K, 5.4K, 6.6K, 8.1K, 9.9K, 12.1K, 14.8K, 18.1K, 22.2K, 27.1K, 33.1K, 49.5K, 60.5K, 74K, 90.5K, 110K, 135K, 165K, 450K). Keywords marked "(rel)" came from the "Related keywords" table on another keyword's page.
- **"WP"** means English Wikipedia user pageviews, all platforms, summed over 12 months (2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30). They come from the [Wikimedia pageviews API](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/metrics/pageviews/per-article/en.wikipedia/all-access/user/Determinant/monthly/20251001/20260930) (swap in each article title). These are a **global interest proxy, not search volume**.
- **"AC-US / AC-IN"** means Google autocomplete, `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us|in&q=…`, fetched 2026-11-03. "IN-only" lists suggestions that appear for gl=in and not for gl=us.
- **"est."** marks my own estimate. Estimates are never presented as measured data.

Calculator queries usually show tools or a Google built-in widget. This needs a manual check in Search Console or an incognito SERP.
