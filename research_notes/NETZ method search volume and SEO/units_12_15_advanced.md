**Sources and labels used in every table**
- **[SD-US] / [SD-IN]**: seodata.dev keyword API (`api.seodata.dev/v1/keyword`). Google monthly search volume for the United States or India, pulled 2026-11-03; some lookups came from seodata's cache with an earlier date, and those say "cached". "similar" means a related keyword that seodata returned in the same response. The anonymous tier allows about 20 lookups a day, so only 25 seed lookups worked and the rest returned HTTP 429. I did not register for the free 500-lookups-a-month tier, because it needs an email sign-up. — [seodata llms.txt](https://seodata.dev/llms.txt)
- **[WP]**: Wikipedia pageviews (en.wikipedia, all-access, human users), total for Oct 2025–Sep 2026, divided by 12 for a monthly figure. This is global English **interest**, not Google search volume. — [Wikimedia pageviews API](https://wikimedia.org/api/rest_v1/metrics/pageviews/per-article/en.wikipedia/all-access/user/Laplace_transform/monthly/20251001/20260930)
- **[AC]**: Google autocomplete (`client=firefox&hl=en&gl=in`), pulled 2026-11-03.
- **[SERP]**: live Google results page (`hl=en&gl=in`) opened in the built-in browser on 2026-11-03. For each page I recorded the top organic results, whether an "AI Overview" appeared, and any "People also ask" questions in the page text. These come from one location at one moment, and Google personalises results.
- **est.** = my estimate or inference, not a measured number.
