## How it works

1. **Do a task** → the first time you get its **medal + 20 💎**.
2. **Do it again** on a later day → **5 💎** (each task pays once a day; 60 💎 a day at most from repeats).
3. **Do any 3 tasks in a day** → daily goal, **+15 💎**.
4. **Do at least 1 task every day** → your **streak** grows by 1. At 1, 5, 10 … 1000 days you get a **streak medal**.
   Miss a day and the streak goes back to 0 (a **streak freeze**, 200 💎, saves it once). Medals you already have stay.
5. **Do tasks on 20 days of a month** → that month's **monthly medal**. Enough monthly medals in a year → the **year badge**.
6. **Pro members** also get **10 Pro tasks** each year → that year's **banner** (3 tasks), **robot skin** (6) and **Pro medal** (10). Kept forever.
7. **Spend gems** in the shop on **robot skins** and **banners**.

Total permanent medals: **50 task + 5 onboarding + 20 streak = 75**.
