# NETZ wins search through the long tail

**Key takeaways.** NETZ can't win the big calculator searches, but it can win thousands of smaller, exam-shaped ones. Every live method page is still titled "NETZ" with the description "current objective: survive" (catalog check, 2026-11-03), so writing one proper title, description, H1 and FAQ per page is the fastest win on offer.

Demand is very uneven:

- **Huge head terms that NETZ can't win:** "unit circle" gets **823K US searches a month**, "quadratic formula" **450K**, "integral calculator" **165K** and "derivative calculator" **135K**. India's "SIP calculator" (**4.09M a month**) and "EMI calculator" (**3.35M**) are bigger still.
- **Long-tail searches NETZ can win:** these are where established sites, Google's own solver and banks are absent or weak. Examples are "by parts formula in integration" (**33.1K India**), "fourier series" (**12.1K India**, the same as the US), "compound interest formula" (**246K India**), "charpit method" (720–880 India, with no AI Overview) and "cayley hamilton theorem calculator" (the only tool found handles 2×2 only).
- **Live pages:** among the existing ✅ pages, the strongest by global interest are Newton-Raphson (325k Wikipedia views a year), Runge-Kutta (234k) and Gauss elimination (172k). The biggest measured US searches are "t test" (40.5K) and "chi square test" (27.1K).

The same pattern holds for AI assistants:

- **AI Overviews are almost everywhere.** One appeared on 29 of the 33 Indian results pages checked.
- **Ranking top 10 is not required to be cited.** Only about 38% of AI Overview citations come from Google's top 10 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/)).
- **What gets a page quoted:** a server-rendered page with a one-line definition, the formula as text, numbered steps, a real worked table and an FAQ.

Two claims in the NETZ planning documents need correcting:

- **MathSolver markup is a site-level feature.** It goes once on the home page and only works if NETZ has a public solver URL. It does not go on each method page.
- **FAQ rich results are gone.** Google removed them on 7 May 2026. A visible FAQ still helps with answers and AI citations, but it won't show as a special result.

The main gap: **India and global keyword volumes are missing for most keywords.** A free seodata.dev key or one Google Keyword Planner session would close it.

## One page template serves every method, with two corrections

The research agrees on a single template. It applies to every method page, old or new. The rest of the report only supplies the words that go into it.

### What goes on every method page

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Correction 1: MathSolver markup goes on the home page, not on each method

Google's MathSolver doc is still live with no deprecation notice ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/math-solvers)). It sets these conditions:

- The markup is added to the **site's home page**.
- It needs a `potentialAction` of type `SolveMathAction` with a **URL template** that accepts the user's expression, plus a privacy-policy `usageInfo` link.
- The first solution and its steps can't sit behind a login.
- Google's question types cover algebra, calculus, matrices, eigenvalues and statistics, but **have no "numerical methods" or "root finding" value**.

So it only pays off if NETZ builds a public solver URL such as `/solve?q={expression}` that renders steps on the server. That is a separate product decision, not a per-page tag.

The catalog's ⭐ row 7 already says this correctly. Some research notes and checklist item 12 still say "`MathSolver` + `LearningResource` on each page". Drop the per-page MathSolver from those.

### Correction 2: FAQ rich results are gone, but on-page FAQs still pay

Google has removed most of the special result types these pages might have used:

- FAQ rich results were cut back to government and health sites in August 2023 and **removed entirely on 7 May 2026**.
- HowTo was removed in September 2023.
- Practice Problem markup was removed on 6 January 2026.
- Google says the removals don't affect ranking ([Schema deprecations timeline](https://patrickstox.com/technical-seo/on-page/structured-data/schema-deprecations/)).

**Don't add `FAQPage`, `HowTo` or Quiz/Practice markup** expecting stars or drop-downs in the results. Keep the visible FAQ section: it answers fan-out queries and gives AI assistants clean question-and-answer text to quote.

### Site-wide setup, done once

**Crawlers (`robots.ts`).** Name the search and retrieval bots explicitly: Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User and Applebot. OpenAI states that "sites that are opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers" ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)). Blocking Claude-SearchBot reduces visibility in Claude ([Anthropic](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518)). PerplexityBot is not used for training ([Perplexity](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots)).

The training bots are a separate business choice for the owner: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended. Blocking them doesn't affect citations in search answers.

The catalog has one detail wrong here. Its row 11 lists Google-Extended as the "Gemini" bot needed for findability. Google-Extended controls AI training and grounding in Gemini apps, not Search. AI Overviews and AI Mode follow Googlebot's rules ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).

Disallow `/api/`, private Board routes and user notes.

**Search Console and Bing.** Set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap with `lastmod` dates to both, and add IndexNow for Bing. Bing has had an AI Performance report since February 2026. It shows citations in Copilot and Bing AI, but not clicks ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report-468751)).

**llms.txt.** It is optional and near-zero value. Google says it has no effect, and vendor logs show AI bots almost never request it ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/llms-txt-adoption-rises-8-8x-but-97-of-files-get-zero-ai-requests/)).

**Analytics.** Build a GA4 "AI assistants" channel using a referrer pattern for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com. Run a monthly test: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the same set of questions, such as "newton raphson method example", and log whether NETZ is cited.

**Launching 250 pages safely.** Google's scaled-content-abuse policy targets "many pages … generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings" with little value ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). 250 method pages are safe when each has its own engine-computed example, table, plot, failure cases and syllabus line. They are risky as one template with the name swapped. Launch in batches, and watch Search Console for "Crawled – currently not indexed".

**Tax and finance pages** need stronger trust signals. Google gives "even more weight" to strong E-E-A-T on topics that affect money ([Google helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)). Each tax and finance page needs:

- a named author and reviewer;
- "Rates as of {date}" with a link to the official source;
- a "Not tax advice" line;
- a method note explaining how the figure is worked out.

## NETZ wins the long tail and loses the head terms

The big calculator searches are held by long-established sites. Symbolab, Wolfram|Alpha, derivative-calculator.net, matrixcalc.org, Omni, calculator.net and Desmos dominate them. Semrush lists derivative-calculator.net at **#1 for "derivative calculator"** ([Semrush](https://ja.semrush.com/website/derivative-calculator.net/overview)), and matrixcalc.org at **#1 for "matrix calculator"** ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/matrixcalc.org/overview/)).

Google also answers some of these searches itself:

- Since 2023 it has had its own step-by-step maths solver ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/01/google-search-update-math-solver-lens/)).
- It has a geometry "Solve for" box on searches such as "area of a circle" ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/3284611?hl=en)).

In India, banks and fintechs hold every finance head term. They include Groww, ClearTax, Bajaj Finserv and BankBazaar.

The research found five kinds of opening where a new, well-built page can rank:

- **Indian textbook phrasing.** India searches for "formula", "class 12", "engineering mathematics", "pdf" and "in hindi" rather than "calculator". The pages ranking for these are university PDFs, Scribd, Studocu and Vedantu: weak, non-interactive pages.
- **Formula-first pages.** In Unit 9 and Unit 10 the formula search is 2–5× bigger than the calculator search. A page that leads with the formula, then the steps, then the tool, fits that intent.
- **"Free full steps".** Symbolab ranks first for "fourier series calculator with steps" but gates the steps behind "Upgrade to Pro" ([Symbolab](https://api.symbolab.com/solver/fourier-series-calculator)).
- **Spam-filled results.** For "gauss seidel method calculator", every result found was a templated spam page with no working tool ([example](https://heartbeatapi.appbase.io/?p=34648)).
- **Spanish-language traffic.** About 40% of matrixcalc.org's visits come from Spain, Mexico, Colombia and Argentina ([Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/matrixcalc.org/overview)), so Spanish pages are a later multiplier.

## Thirteen live slugs need new addresses, with permanent redirects

Google treats URLs as case-sensitive. Several live slugs have capitals, misspellings or a stray "-s-". Each fix needs four things:

- a permanent redirect from the old path: in Next.js, `redirects()` with `permanent: true` sends a 308, which Google treats like a 301;
- the old URL removed from the sitemap;
- internal links updated;
- a self-referencing canonical on the new URL.

Add middleware that lowercases any capitalised path, and keep a `slugAliases` map in the method registry so later renames redirect automatically. NETZ runs a newer Next.js than most guides describe. Check `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` for the current `redirects` and middleware APIs before coding (project rule in `Netz/AGENTS.md`).

## Unit 9: one page per shape beats one big calculator

In geometry, **the formula search is bigger than the calculator search** ([seodata.dev](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/area-of-circle)). "Area of a circle" (165K US), "distance formula" (165K), "slope formula" (90.5K) and "midpoint formula" (90.5K) all beat their calculator terms. The general hub term "area calculator" is only 27.1K, and much of it is land-measurement intent.

So build **one page per shape**, covering area plus perimeter (2D) or volume plus surface area (3D). Add two hubs that link to them: `/area-perimeter-calculator` and `/volume-surface-area-calculator`. Omni uses the same one-URL-per-shape pattern ([Omni](https://www.omnicalculator.com/math/cone-volume)).

The unit circle (**823K US**) is the biggest single term in the catalog. People want a chart and a tool, so lead with an interactive, printable chart.

Expect zero-click results on plain formula searches: Google shows its own geometry "Solve for" box ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/3284611?hl=en)). NETZ's edge is the worked steps and a labelled diagram, which a snippet can't show.

India searches these topics differently. It uses "ka formula", "class 9/10", "NCERT" and "trapezium" (where the US says "trapezoid"). It also searches combined-solid problems ("cone and hemisphere"). The distance and slope pages should open with "coordinate geometry" so they don't compete with physics intent.

## Conclusion

The research shows that NETZ's search problem is mostly its own pages, not the market. Thirty-four live pages carry the same title and a joke description, and thirteen of their URLs need new addresses because of capitals, misspellings or names nobody searches. On top of that, the planning documents still assume per-page MathSolver markup and FAQ stars, and neither exists any more.

Fixing those costs days, not months. It matters most on the pages where competition is weakest, because there a correctly labelled page can rank on relevance alone: Weddle, Gauss forward/backward, Stirling, Bessel, Gauss-Seidel and the Indian PDE topics.

There is a second, less obvious finding. The searches NETZ can win and the searches AI assistants answer are now the same searches. Formula, "solved example" and syllabus-phrased questions trigger AI Overviews almost every time, and those Overviews increasingly cite pages outside the top 10.

So a server-rendered page that puts the formula, the worked table and a plain FAQ in its HTML is built for both Google and ChatGPT at once. A calculator-only page that renders in the browser reaches neither. That makes the build order clear: fix the 34 live pages, ship the formula-first pages for the Indian long tails, and leave the 100K-plus calculator heads until the site has earned links.
